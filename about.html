Our Story

Friends Of Planet Uganda is an accidental environmental sustainability initiative born out of patriotism and a deep concern for our planet's future. What started as a small group of passionate individuals has grown into a movement dedicated to protecting and restoring Uganda's natural environment.

We immerse ourselves in practices of sustaining the environment through research, sensitization, tree planting, waste management, emissions control, and urban farming. Our approach is hands-on, community-driven, and focused on creating lasting change.

Our journey began with a simple realization: that we must act now to protect our environment for future generations. Today, we continue to expand our reach, touching more communities and implementing more projects that make a real difference.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To inspire and empower communities to protect and restore the natural environment through sensitization and hands-on conservation efforts for the next generation to inherit a cleaner, greener and eco-friendly planet.

Our Vision

To be a continent leader in environment restoration and climate resilience for cleaner air, water and a green planet. We envision a future where communities live in harmony with nature, where sustainable practices are the norm, and where environmental stewardship is everyone's responsibility.

Our Approach

Research-Driven Solutions

We base our interventions on solid research and data to ensure effectiveness and sustainability. Our team continuously studies environmental challenges and develops evidence-based solutions.

Community Engagement

We work hand-in-hand with local communities, understanding their unique challenges and co-creating solutions that work for their specific context. Community ownership is key to our success.

Hands-On Conservation

We believe in leading by example. Our team is actively involved in tree planting, clean-up activities, and environmental restoration projects.

Partnership Building

We collaborate with government agencies, NGOs, private sector, and international organizations to amplify our impact and create synergistic solutions.
