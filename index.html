About FOP-Uganda

Friends Of Planet Uganda is an accidental environmental sustainability initiative based on patriotism. We have immersed ourselves in practices of sustaining the environment, such as research, sensitization,tree planting, waste management, emissions control, and urban farming.

We are committed to continuing to engage in such practices, and we believe they can help mitigate the climate change effects, hence creating a clean and green environment for us all.

Promoting environmental sustainability, forest preservation, and waste management solutions.

Our Mission

To inspire and empower communities to protect and restore the natural environment through sensitization and hands on conservation efforts for the next generation to inherit a cleaner, greener and eco-friendly planet.

Our Vision

To be a continent leader in environment restoration and climate resilience for cleaner air, water and green planet.
